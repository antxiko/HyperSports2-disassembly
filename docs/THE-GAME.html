The game

Three events, in this order, written into the ROM at 0x4791 as three rows of sixteen tiles each: SKEET SHOOTING, ARCHERY and WEIGHT LIFTING. The byte at 0xE080 says which one is running, and its two low bits index every table that splits by event.

Skeet shooting

Two crosshairs and a clock. LANZA_UN_PLATO (0x5738) releases a clay every 0x18 frames into whichever of the three slots at 0xE100 is free; each slot is sixteen bytes with the clay's posture, its 16-bit X and Y and its two speeds. CAEN_LOS_PLATOS adds gravity, and the gravity is bigger the higher the clay is, so the arc tightens as it falls.

The trigger fires the top barrel with bit 3 of the joystick and the bottom one with bits 2 and 4. MIRA_LOS_IMPACTOS (0x58E3) walks the three slots and, for each pellet, allows six points of slack in X and six in Y — and only postures 0 to 3 can be broken.

Archery

The readouts are three, and the screen names them: ANGLE on row 10, WIND at the bottom and SCORE beside it.

  • The angle rises 0.28 degrees on each press of the button and wraps round at 4.00 (0x5B42, in BCD, with the hundreds byte compared against 4).
  • The wind is not random either: SIGUIENTE_VIENTO (0x5BB8) walks sixteen values at 0x5BCB in a ring, bit 7 for the side and the low nibble for the strength. As the arrow rises, CORRIGE_LA_DERIVA (0x5B8C) bends it one point per stretch of the table at 0x5BB0.
  • The hit is decided by QUE_ANILLO_HA_TOCADO (0x5CCF) against a target centred on X 0xBC and Y 0x53 — the same two constants you can see on the screen — and each ring pays what the ten-word BCD table at 0x5D37 says: 10, 100, 170, 210, 250, 290, 320, 350, 400 and 600.

Weight lifting

First you pick a class. ELIGE_LA_CATEGORIA (0x75F7) steps through eight of them with any direction and takes it with the button; PINTA_LA_CATEGORIA (0x756D) writes its name, fourteen tiles from 0x7587, into the CHOOSE CLASS sign, and PINTA_LOS_KILOS (0x6F25) puts its weight in the KG board: 150, 165, 180, 195, 210, 225, 240 and 260 kilos.

Then the POWER bar on row 23 (0x6E24), which the table at 0x6E77 drains every frame and the joystick feeds. The bar rises through the 36 heights at 0x6ED4, and once it is up, three judges' lights come on in column 2, one every 0x20 frames (0x6F78). At the third the lift is good, and 0x6FA2 drops the two plates of the barbell from Y 0x4A, two points per frame.

The scoreboard, and what carries over

The top of every screen is the same: SCORE, HI, YOU, WORLD RECORD, QUALIFY and the three 1st TRY / 2nd TRY / 3rd TRY rows, painted from the stream at 0x4749 with the label list at 0x4712 over it. Points are three bytes of BCD at 0xE063 and top out at 999999.

After the third event comes the records screen, and then the cycle starts again: 0xE080 counts 0, 1, 2, 3 and at 3 the table of records takes over — except that bit 2 of the same byte skips it every other round.

Hyper Sports 2 was published by Konami for the MSX; its catalogue number is RC-717 and it is 16 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
