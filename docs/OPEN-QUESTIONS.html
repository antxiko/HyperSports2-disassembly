Open questions

The binary is explained down to the last byte and it reassembles into the ROM. That does not mean everything is understood. What is still open, said plainly:

The archer's six sprite records

COLOCA_LAS_SEIS_FICHAS (0x5E4D) copies the 24 bytes at 0x5E59 into 0xE0C4 with no arithmetic at all: six sprite records with X 0xE4 and 0xE8, at the right edge of the screen, and patterns 0x64, 0x68, 0x6C, 0x70 and 0x18. Drawn from the ROM, 0x68 and 0x74 are a figure with its arms out, and 0x70 and 0x78 look like legs; ALTERNA_DOS_PATRONES (0x5E35) swaps between the two pairs every eight frames. It looks like a figure running, but the moment it appears on screen has not been caught, so this site does not claim what it is.

What 0xE075 holds

ELIGE_LA_CATEGORIA (0x75F7) walks the eight weight classes in a ring, and when it wraps it does not go back to zero: it takes the value at 0xE075. Which class that is, and who writes it, is not settled.

The two motors of the archery

0xE147 picks between two routines that move the same thing: AVANZA_Y_REMATA (0x5DE8) and ANDA_DE_UN_LADO_A_OTRO (0x5BDB). Both are commented for what they do, one instruction at a time, but which one runs in which situation has not been watched happening.

The fourth graphics record and the third background

The table at 0x6C3A has four records of three pointers; the one at 0x42A0, which holds the name-table backgrounds, has three words. The fourth graphics record is the records screen, which never asks for a background — but if 0xE080 & 3 ever reached 3 with CAMBIA_DE_PRUEBA running, the background table would be read one word past its end, at 0x42A6, which is the first instruction of APAGA_EL_PRIMER_SPRITE. Reading the state machine it does not look reachable: the bit 2 test at 0x41FB bumps 0xE080 past that value first. It has not been proved by running it.

What the demo does

Bit 6 of 0xE002 marks the demo, and 0xE1AB makes a shot only count without breaking anything. How long the demo lasts, and what it plays, has not been timed.

The name of the third of the three alphabets

tools/textos.py reads the cartridge's text with three tile bases: 0x21, 0x51 and 0xD1 all stand for A. The first two are the two halves of the scoreboard font; where the third one is used has not been pinned down beyond the title screen's PUSH SPACE KEY TO START.

If you can close any of these, the listing is set up so that an answer is one line in src/hypersports2.notes and a make.

Hyper Sports 2 was published by Konami for the MSX; its catalogue number is RC-717 and it is 16 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
